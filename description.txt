[h1]Coalition Demands[/h1]
Coalitions fight to undo your conquests: they take back the locations you seized from their members and neighbours, free your new vassals and revive the countries you destroyed. No humiliation.

[h2]Who it's for[/h2]
This mod is about player fun, not historical outcomes. It makes blobbing harder, for players and the AI alike: what you take, a coalition can take back. In return, antagonism no longer lingers almost forever: giving your conquests back, or beating a coalition, wipes most of it.

[h2]Return Conquests[/h2]
A new peace treaty for a coalition that wins its war. It costs a flat 75% war score; the AI always asks for it, and accepts it when you offer it.
[list]
[*]Locations you took from coalition members go back to them.
[*]Locations you took from countries outside the coalition go back too, if they lie near it (same continent as a member's capital, or bordering a member). Coalitions often form exactly while you hold a truce with your victims, who can't join.
[*]Countries you destroyed near the coalition rise again.
[*]Lands you passed to your own vassals count as well, e.g. a vassal you made from conquered land.
[*]Subjects you made recently go back to the overlord you took them from, or become independent.
[*]Only recent conquests: 30 years by default, and not once the location has become your core.
[*]Antagonism from those conquests is taken off: each returned location and freed subject drops the antagonism it caused (new "Returned conquered locations" and "Freed subjects" lines).
[/list]

[h2]Coalition wars[/h2]
[list]
[*]No Humiliation in coalition wars.
[*]Members want their own cores back through the game's Return Core treaty, and nothing else.
[*]The strongest member in the war leads it (the game's own country strength), whoever declared it.
[*]Winning with Dissolve Coalition now also removes 75% of the losers' antagonism towards you.
[/list]

[h2]Who joins coalitions[/h2]
Besides the usual countries with antagonism above 50 and opinion below -50:
[list]
[*]Countries that chose the coalition's target as their rival. Optionally, only once the coalition is strong enough.
[*]A hegemon against a neighbouring hegemon (borders within 300), if its opinion of it is below +150.
[*]The Holy Roman Emperor, when a member of the Empire is in the coalition, if its opinion of the target is below +150.
[*]Countries under a truce with the target, when their antagonism is above 100 and the coalition is more than twice as strong as the target. Rivals, hegemons and the Emperor always keep a truce.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Everything above can be tuned in the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692202776]Community Mod Framework[/url] mod menu (required): the years a conquest can be demanded back, whether a core stops it, whether non-core locations go back too (off by default), the truce rule with its antagonism and strength thresholds, each of the extra joining rules, and an optional minimum coalition strength for rivals (off by default).

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Can be added to a running game, but conquests made before that aren't recorded and can't be demanded back.
[*]A change of the years setting applies to new conquests only.
[*]Changes these game files, so mods that change the same ones may conflict: peace_treaties/humiliate.txt, peace_treaties/dissolve_coalition.txt, casus_belli/coalition.txt, international_organizations/coalition.txt, scripted_triggers/international_organization_triggers.txt.
[*]English and Russian text.
[/list]
